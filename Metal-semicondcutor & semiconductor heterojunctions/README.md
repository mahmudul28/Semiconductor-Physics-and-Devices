## 🖥️ Featured Simulators

Once you open the dashboard, you will be able to launch and interact with:

* **[Heterojunction Simulator](./heterojunction_simulator.html):** Visualize abrupt Al<sub>0.3</sub>Ga<sub>0.7</sub>As-GaAs heterojunctions. Explore band bending, spike/notch formations, and 2DEG accumulation layers across various doping profiles.
* **[Schottky Diode Simulator](./schottky_diode_simulator.html):** Analyze rectifying metal-semiconductor contacts. Adjust metal work functions and semiconductor doping to observe barrier heights (Φ<sub>B0</sub>), built-in potentials, and bias effects.
* **[Ohmic Contact Simulator](./ohmic_contact_simulator.html):** Simulate non-rectifying contacts. Visualize ideal accumulation layers & degenerate ultra-thin tunneling barriers used for low-resistance connections.
