## 🖥️ Featured Simulators

Once you open the dashboard, you will be able to launch and interact with:

* **Heterojunction Simulator:** Visualize abrupt $\text{Al}_{0.3}\text{Ga}_{0.7}\text{As}\text{–}\text{GaAs}$ heterojunctions. Explore band bending, spike/notch formations, and 2DEG accumulation layers across various doping profiles.
* **Schottky Diode Simulator:** Analyze rectifying metal-semiconductor contacts. Adjust metal work functions and semiconductor doping to observe barrier heights ($\Phi_{\text{B0}}$), built-in potentials, and bias effects.
* **Ohmic Contact Simulator:** Simulate non-rectifying contacts. Visualize ideal accumulation layers and degenerate ultra-thin tunneling barriers used for low-resistance connections.
