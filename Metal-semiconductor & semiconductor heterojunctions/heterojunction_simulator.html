How to read this diagram

Each dropdown sets the doping level of a region. N/P (uppercase) selects the wide-gap Al₀.₃Ga₀.₇As side; n/p (lowercase) selects the narrow-gap GaAs side — pick any combination on either side to build a homojunction or heterojunction.

  • E_F is pinned flat (the equilibrium condition) — every other level is drawn relative to it.
  • Band offsets ΔE_c and ΔE_v are fixed by the two materials' electron affinities and gaps (Anderson's rule) and don't change with doping.
  • Band bending width on each side scales inversely with doping strength — a lightly doped (or intrinsic) region bends gradually over a wide depletion width; a degenerate (++) region bends sharply right at the interface.
  • V_bi, the built-in potential, is the total vacuum-level drop needed to align the two bulk regions — it is split across the junction in inverse proportion to each side's doping.
