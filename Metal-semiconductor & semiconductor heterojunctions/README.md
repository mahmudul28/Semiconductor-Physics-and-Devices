## 🖥️ Featured Simulators

Once you open the dashboard, you will be able to launch and interact with:

* **[Heterojunction Simulator](https://mahmudul28.github.io/Semiconductor-Physics-and-Devices/Metal-semiconductor%20%26%20semiconductor%20heterojunctions/heterojunction_simulator.html):** Visualize abrupt Al<sub>0.3</sub>Ga<sub>0.7</sub>As-GaAs heterojunctions. Explore band bending, spike/notch formations, and 2DEG accumulation layers across various doping profiles.
* **[Schottky Diode Simulator](https://mahmudul28.github.io/Semiconductor-Physics-and-Devices/Metal-semiconductor%20%26%20semiconductor%20heterojunctions/schottky_diode_simulator.html):** Analyze rectifying metal-semiconductor contacts. Adjust metal work functions and semiconductor doping to observe barrier heights (Φ<sub>B0</sub>), built-in potentials, and bias effects.
* **[Ohmic Contact Simulator](https://mahmudul28.github.io/Semiconductor-Physics-and-Devices/Metal-semiconductor%20%26%20semiconductor%20heterojunctions/ohmic_contact_simulator.html):** Simulate non-rectifying contacts. Visualize ideal accumulation layers and degenerate ultra-thin tunneling barriers used for low-resistance connections.
