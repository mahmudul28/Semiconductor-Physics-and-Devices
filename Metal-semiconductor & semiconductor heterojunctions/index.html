How the three relate: all three tools plot the same quantity — E_c(x) and E_v(x) across a junction — but for a different pairing: two semiconductors with different band gaps (heterojunction), a metal and a lightly doped semiconductor that rectifies (Schottky), and a metal and a heavily doped semiconductor that doesn't (ohmic).
