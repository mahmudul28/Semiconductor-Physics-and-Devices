Physics Concepts

• Increasing VDS increases reverse bias at the drain end of the gate junction.
• The depletion region expands most widely near the drain.
• When VDS = VGS - Vp, the channel "pinches off" (width approaches zero).
• Further increases in VDS drop across the depleted region (ΔL), leaving current roughly constant.
• In saturation, the pinch-off point moves towards the source as VDS increases.
• The effective channel length (L) decreases slightly (L - ΔL).
• Since resistance is proportional to length, shorter L means higher current.
• Modeled as ID = ID(ideal) * (1 + λVDS), creating a non-zero slope (output conductance, gd).
• At high electric fields (short channels), carrier velocity stops increasing linearly with field (mobility degrades).
• Velocity approaches a limit (vsat).
• Causes current to saturate at lower voltages and values than square-law predicts.
• Transfer curves become more linear rather than quadratic.
