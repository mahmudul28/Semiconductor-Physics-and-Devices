Depletion & pinch-off, in words
Reverse bias across the gate-channel junction widens the depletion region h(x) as it grows from source to drain. When the local reverse bias reaches the pinch-off voltage, the depletion layers meet and the channel is squeezed to zero thickness — carriers are swept through the resulting high-field ΔL region rather than drifting normally.
